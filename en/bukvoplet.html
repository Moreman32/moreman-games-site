Bukvoplet is a vibrant word puzzle where you connect letters, discover words, find bonus combinations, and unlock new levels.

Complete engaging challenges, collect coins, use hints and a hammer when a level gets tricky, earn random rewards, and build your own collection of fascinating facts.

Features

  • More than 5,000 levels
  • 250 fascinating facts with illustrations
  • Simple and intuitive word-building gameplay
  • Bonus words and rewards for attentive players
  • Hints and a hammer for challenging levels
  • Daily rewards, lucky spin, and gift chests
  • Bright fantasy-inspired visuals and smooth animations
  • Perfect for both short sessions and long playtime

Connect letters, uncover hidden words, and progress through the levels at your own pace.

Every completed chapter unlocks a new fact about nature, animals, history, science, culture, and the world around us.

If you want to relax, train your attention, and learn something new, Bukvoplet is easy to pick up and satisfying to play.
