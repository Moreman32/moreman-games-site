Support — Cyprus Explore

Cyprus Explore is an app by Moreman Games. If you need help, want to report a bug, or submit a data-related request, write to us:

Support email: support@moreman.games

Typical response time: 3-5 business days.

How we can help

  • launch issues, freezes, or crashes;
  • incorrect beach or landmark data;
  • authentication issues (Email, Google, Apple);
  • map, geolocation, or visit-verification errors;
  • questions about photo uploads, reviews, or achievements;
  • privacy and data deletion requests.

What to include in your message

  • app name: Cyprus Explore;
  • device model and OS version (iOS / Android);
  • a brief description of the issue;
  • a screenshot or reproduction steps if possible.

Data Deletion Request

To delete your account and all associated data (photos, reviews, visit history), send an email to support@moreman.games with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

Please include: the app name, platform, device, and the email linked to your account. This helps us process your request faster.
